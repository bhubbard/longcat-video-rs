longcat-video-rs — Unified Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video & Long-Video DiT in Rust

longcat-video-rs is a pure Rust inference engine for the 13.6B LongCat-Video Diffusion Transformer supporting text-to-video, image-to-video, and minute-long video continuation. It uses 3D-RoPE positional encodings, block-causal attention caching, and coarse-to-fine temporal upscaling to generate coherent 720p/1080p video without temporal drift.

01 // Partitioned 3D-RoPE (16T+24H+24W)

Partitions each 64-D attention head into 16 temporal, 24 vertical, and 24 horizontal rotary frequency channels, applying norm-preserving complex rotations across 3D video grids. 

let rope = RoPE3D::new(RoPE3DConfig::default());
let rot = rope.apply_rope(&token_64d, t, h, w);

02 // Shifted Rectified Flow ODE

Applies high-resolution time shifting $\sigma_{\text{shifted}} = \frac{s \cdot t}{1 + (s - 1)t}$ to allocate more Euler integration steps to high-noise structural formation (`step_cfg`). 

let sched = FlowMatchingEulerScheduler::new(3.0);
let steps = sched.get_schedule(30);
let next = FlowMatchingEulerScheduler::
    step_cfg(&x, &vc, &vu, 5.0, steps[0].dt);
